How it works

Pick a length and tap 출발. Your party starts walking. They keep walking for exactly as long as you stay out of the app. Every five leagues they run into something; clearing it pays spoils, and spoils become ember when the expedition comes home. Ember recruits and promotes the party, and a bigger party gets deeper before it starts losing fights.

Why did my run end?

Opening another app ends the run if you stay away longer than the grace window (15 seconds). A lost run still pays 40% of the spoils you had collected, and the minutes you focused are still counted in your totals — the expedition can fail, but the focus always counts.

Can I lock my phone during a session?

Yes, and it is the best thing you can do. Locking the screen and putting the phone down is not treated as leaving — the march continues and the time counts. Only switching to another app ends a run.

iOS gives no single reliable signal that separates "screen locked" from "opened another app", so Nightwalk combines two: whether protected data became unavailable (accurate on any device with a passcode) and whether screen brightness dropped to zero. When both readings are inconclusive the run is kept, never lost. If you ever see a run survive when you think it should not have, that is this rule doing its job — and we would still like to hear about it.

My party keeps losing fights

The lone lantern bearer clears the first encounter or two and then starts falling behind, which is intended. Spend ember on the warden first — they take the damage at the front of the line so the others keep walking — then the scout and the finder for the spoils bonus.

Does a fallen party member end the run?

No. A member at zero stamina stops contributing their spoils bonus and gets dragged along, but the expedition never wipes and the session keeps running. Nothing in Nightwalk punishes you for focusing longer.

Does the screen stay on?

The automatic screen timeout is suppressed while a session is running, so the march stays visible if you leave the phone face up. Locking it yourself still works normally, and the timeout goes back to your system setting the moment the session ends.

Where is my data?

On your device only. See the privacy policy. There is no account and no sync, so deleting the app erases your history permanently — there is no way for us to restore it.

Bug reports

Email richohios@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing. If a session ended when it should not have, telling us whether the screen was locked or you had switched apps is the single most useful detail.
