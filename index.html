NIGHTWALK

A focus timer where the expedition only advances while you do.

Start a session and a party sets out across the dark, lit by one lantern. They walk for exactly as long as you stay out of your phone. Leave the app and they stop; stay away and they lose the road.

What makes it different

Timer apps that grow a tree give you a tree. Nightwalk spends the time instead: focused minutes are the resource the expedition runs on, encounters cost stamina, and what survives the walk buys the next party.

Three rules it keeps

  • Nothing to tap. There is no input at all during a session — that is the whole idea.
  • Locking your phone is not leaving. Screen off, face down, still walking.
  • Focusing longer is never worse. Lost encounters still pay, the party cannot wipe, and every session counts the minutes you actually concentrated — even the ones that ended badly.
