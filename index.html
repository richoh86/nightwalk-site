Focus timer · Productivity

The expedition only advances while you do.

Start a session and a party sets out across the dark, lit by one lantern. They walk for exactly as long as you stay out of your phone. Leave the app and they stop. Stay away and they lose the road.

iPhone · Free · Works offline

The descent

A run that lasts more than one session.

A descent spans several sessions. When a session ends the party makes camp at the depth it reached, and the next session breaks camp and walks on from there. Choose “Return home” at camp and everything the party is carrying is banked, along with a bonus for how deep it went.

Nothing to tap

You are not supposed to be touching your phone.

There are no buttons, no choices and no controls during a session — that is the entire point. The screen is built to be read in one second from across a desk.

The count

Focusing longer is never worse.

Lost encounters still pay. The party cannot be wiped out. Every summary leads with the minutes you actually concentrated — even the sessions that ended badly. The expedition can fail; the focus always counts.

What you get, and what you don’t

Locking is not leaving

Screen off and face down is the best thing you can do — the march continues.

Works offline

No network code in the app at all.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no sync, no profile.

Nothing to buy

No ads, no in-app purchases.

Spoils that make it home become ember, and ember recruits and promotes your party.
