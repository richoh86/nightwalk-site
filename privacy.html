PRIVACY

Last updated 14 August 2026

Nightwalk collects nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, no account system and no network code in the app at all.

What the app stores

Everything Nightwalk remembers is written to your device's local application storage (UserDefaults) and never leaves it:

  • your session log — for each finished session, the date, minutes focused, depth reached, spoils, ember and whether the expedition returned
  • your party roster and its ranks
  • your ember balance and total focused time
  • your preferred session length

This matches the app's privacy manifest, which declares exactly one accessed API category: UserDefaults, for reason CA92.1 — data accessible only to the app itself. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the app does not do

Nightwalk does not use the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, calendar, location, health data, motion sensors or notifications. It does not read which other apps you have or use. The session timer works entirely from the clock and from whether Nightwalk itself is in the foreground — it cannot see what you do in other apps, only that you left this one.

What we receive

Nothing. We have no server, and the app makes no network requests. If you email us for support, we will of course have whatever you choose to put in that email; we use it only to answer you and we do not add you to any list.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13. There is no advertising and no third-party content.

Third parties

The app bundles no third-party SDKs. Apple may collect standard App Store information when you download or update the app; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
